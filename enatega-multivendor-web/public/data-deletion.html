Section 01

How to request deletion

Email privacy@zetahub.co.uk with the subject NextHop data deletion request. Include:

  • your name;
  • the email address connected to your account;
  • your phone or WhatsApp number, including country code;
  • your role or account type, such as customer, restaurant partner or rider; and
  • a relevant order or restaurant reference, if available.
Keep sensitive information out of your email. Never send passwords, access tokens, full payment card numbers or identity documents unless NextHop specifically requests a proportionate verification method.
Section 02

Identity verification

To protect your information and prevent fraudulent deletion, we may ask for limited additional information to confirm your identity and connection to the account, number or order. We will use a proportionate method and only ask for what is reasonably necessary.

Section 03

Timing

We normally respond within one month in line with applicable UK data-protection requirements. If a request is complex or we receive multiple requests from you, the law may allow us to extend that period. If so, we will tell you within the initial period and explain the reason and expected timing.

We may also need clarification before we can locate the relevant account or records. The response period may not begin until identity is verified where verification is reasonably required.

Section 04

What we delete or anonymise

Subject to lawful exceptions and depending on how you used NextHop, deletion may cover:

  • your account and profile details;
  • saved delivery or collection addresses;
  • WhatsApp and customer-support messages associated with you where NextHop controls them;
  • device and notification identifiers; and
  • other personal data that is no longer required for an operational or lawful purpose.

Where complete deletion is not necessary or technically appropriate, we may irreversibly anonymise information so it no longer identifies you.

Section 05

What we may retain

Some records may need to be retained for a limited period, including order, payment, accounting and tax records; fraud and security logs; records connected to disputes or chargebacks; and information required to meet legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Backups may retain information until they are overwritten through normal rotation. Any retained information is restricted to the relevant purpose and is not used as an active customer profile.

Section 06

Restaurant-controlled data

A restaurant may independently control information about its customers and orders. Where NextHop processes information solely for a restaurant, we may coordinate the request with that restaurant or direct you to it so the appropriate organisation can respond. A restaurant’s legal record-keeping duties may differ from ours.

Section 07

WhatsApp: separate actions

Deleting a WhatsApp chat from your phone does not itself delete records held on NextHop servers. Likewise, blocking a business in WhatsApp or deleting your WhatsApp account is separate from sending NextHop a deletion request.

Meta and WhatsApp control information within their own services. Their own settings, terms and deletion processes apply to that information.

Section 08

Confirmation and escalation

When we complete the request, we will confirm the outcome or explain any information we must retain and why. For more detail about your data-protection rights, read our Privacy Policy.

If you are unhappy with our response, reply to the request email so we can review it. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

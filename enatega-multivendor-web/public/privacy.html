Section 01

Who we are and scope

NextHop is a multi-vendor restaurant ordering and delivery platform available through zetahub.co.uk, mobile applications and WhatsApp-powered ordering experiences. This policy applies to customers, restaurant partners, delivery riders, administrators, website and app visitors, and people who communicate with a restaurant through a NextHop-powered WhatsApp bot.

Depending on the interaction, NextHop may decide how and why personal information is processed, or process information on behalf of a restaurant partner. A restaurant may also act independently and provide its own terms or privacy notice. This policy is informational and does not replace or alter those restaurant-specific notices.

Section 02

Information we collect

The information we handle depends on how you use NextHop and may include:

  • Identity and contact details, such as name, email address, telephone number and account role.
  • Account information, including credentials stored in hashed form, preferences and saved addresses.
  • Order information, including baskets, menu choices, add-ons, delivery instructions, totals, payment status, order history and support enquiries.
  • Address and location information, including delivery addresses, approximate or precise customer location where permission is granted, and rider location during delivery.
  • Restaurant information, including business contact details, address, menus, opening information and order operations.
  • WhatsApp and customer-service information, including message content, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, delivery status and related metadata.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, device and browser details, app logs, push notification identifiers, diagnostic events, cookie data and analytics where enabled.
Section 03

WhatsApp and conversational ordering

When you message a restaurant number connected to NextHop, WhatsApp sends message content and metadata to our webhook so that the service can interpret your request, show menu options, manage a basket, create or track an order and respond to you. Messages may also be sent to provide operational updates, such as order acceptance, preparation, rider assignment and delivery status.

Meta and WhatsApp process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to those policies. A restaurant may have access to conversations and order information relating to its own customers.

Section 04

How we use information and our lawful bases

We use information to:

  • create accounts and authenticate users;
  • display menus and enable ordering, payment and delivery;
  • connect customers, restaurants and riders;
  • provide order updates, notifications and customer support;
  • maintain safety, prevent misuse and troubleshoot the service;
  • improve performance and understand service usage; and
  • meet applicable legal, tax, accounting or regulatory duties.

Under UK data protection law, our lawful bases may include performing a contract with you, pursuing legitimate interests in operating and improving a reliable delivery platform, complying with legal obligations, and obtaining consent where required. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider the impact on your rights and expectations.

Section 05

When we share information

We share information only where needed to operate the service, fulfil an order, comply with law or protect people and the platform. Recipients may include:

  • the restaurant handling an order and the assigned rider;
  • operational service providers for cloud hosting, databases, mapping, notifications, customer communications and analytics;
  • Meta/WhatsApp for conversational messaging, and payment providers such as Stripe;
  • professional advisers, regulators, courts or authorities where reasonably required; and
  • parties involved in a genuine business reorganisation, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information.

Section 06

Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe. These providers receive and process payment information under their own terms and privacy notices. NextHop should not receive or store your full payment card number. We may receive limited payment information, such as payment status, amount, transaction reference and limited card details needed for receipts, support or fraud prevention.

Section 07

Location information

Location helps us find nearby restaurants, validate delivery areas, estimate routes and coordinate deliveries. With device permission, customer apps may provide approximate or precise coordinates. Riders may share current location while online or completing an active delivery so that delivery progress can be coordinated and shown to authorised participants.

You can control app location permission through your device settings, although disabling it may limit location-dependent features. You can usually enter an address manually where that option is available.

Section 08

Cookies, analytics and device data

NextHop services may use necessary local storage or cookies to support sign-in, security, preferences and core functionality. Analytics or similar technologies may be used where enabled and, when required, with your consent. Mobile apps may use device identifiers, diagnostics and push notification tokens for performance, security and notifications.

This privacy policy page itself does not set cookies, run analytics or load third-party tracking resources.
Section 09

Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, keeping necessary business records and meeting legal obligations. Retention varies by record type, account status, operational need and applicable law. Information may be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer required.

Section 10

Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information, including access controls, credential hashing, service monitoring and restricted access to operational systems. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Keep your credentials private and contact us promptly if you believe your account or communications have been compromised.

Section 11

International transfers

Some technology providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data protection law requires it, we use appropriate transfer mechanisms or safeguards, such as adequacy regulations or approved contractual protections, and consider the circumstances of the transfer.

Section 12

Your choices and UK data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict processing, object to processing, and receive certain information in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We encourage you to contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a rights request, and some rights are subject to legal exceptions.

Section 13

Children

NextHop is not designed for children to use independently where they cannot lawfully enter into the relevant transaction or provide necessary consent. Parents and guardians should supervise a child's use of ordering and delivery services. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

Section 14

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as NextHop develops, our providers change or legal requirements evolve. We will post the updated version here and change the effective or last-updated date. Where appropriate, we may provide an additional notice through the service.
